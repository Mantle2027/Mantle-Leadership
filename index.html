GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the programme for?

It is proposed for young adults aged 18–30+ who are involved in their local church, exploring ministry or developing leadership potential.

When and where will sessions take place?

The proposed sessions run on selected Saturday mornings, 10:00 am–12:00 pm, from January to April 2027 at Bethel Gibson Road Church. Dates remain subject to confirmation.

What will each session involve?

Each session will include biblical teaching, mentoring wisdom, a workshop, practical discussion and an assignment for the following session.
